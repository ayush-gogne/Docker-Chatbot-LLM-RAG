Launch the lab
Start the labspace:
$
docker compose -p labspace -f oci://dockersamples/labspace-creating-ai-product-reviewer up -d
Open your browser to
http://localhost:3030

When you're done, tear down the labspace:
$
docker compose -p labspace down
